MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between ABC Technologies and XYZ Solutions

1. Scope of Services
Vendor will provide services described in mutually agreed statements of work.

2. Fees and Payment
ABC will pay undisputed invoices within thirty days.

3. Limitation of Liability
The aggregate liability of either party shall not exceed fees paid or payable during the twelve months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

4. Intellectual Property
All deliverables specifically developed for ABC under this Agreement shall vest in ABC upon payment. Vendor retains ownership of its pre-existing materials.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall protect confidential information and limit disclosure to authorised representatives with a need to know.

6. Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify ABC against third-party claims arising from Vendor negligence, wilful misconduct or infringement of third-party IP rights.

7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured thirty days after written notice.

8. Governing Law
The governing law and dispute forum shall be mutually agreed in writing and approved by ABC legal.
