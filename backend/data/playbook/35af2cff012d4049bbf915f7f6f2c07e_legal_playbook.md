# ABC Technologies Legal Negotiation Playbook

## RULE_001 - Liability
Policy: Vendor liability should generally be capped at fees paid or payable during the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Any exception requires legal approval.
Red flags:
- Unlimited liability
- No financial liability cap
- Broad carve-outs from the cap
Preferred wording: The aggregate liability of either party shall not exceed the fees paid or payable under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim, except for exclusions expressly approved in writing by ABC's legal team.
Fallback wording: Fallback position requires legal approval.
Applicable documents: NDA, MSA
Escalation: Vendor rejects a reasonable cap or requests broad uncapped liability.

## RULE_002 - Intellectual Property
Policy: Deliverables specifically created for ABC under the agreement should be assigned to ABC, subject to clearly identified vendor background IP.
Red flags:
- Vendor owns all project deliverables
- No assignment of newly created IP
- Broad licence-back without restrictions
Preferred wording: All intellectual property rights in deliverables specifically created for ABC under this Agreement shall vest in ABC upon payment of applicable fees. Vendor retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, subject to a licence sufficient for ABC to use the deliverables.
Fallback wording: Fallback position requires legal approval.
Applicable documents: MSA
Escalation: Vendor refuses assignment of bespoke deliverables.

## RULE_003 - Confidentiality
Policy: Each receiving party must protect confidential information and disclose it only to authorised representatives with a need to know, subject to equivalent confidentiality duties.
Red flags:
- No confidentiality obligations
- Unrestricted disclosure rights
- No safeguards or return/deletion obligations
Preferred wording: The receiving party shall protect confidential information using reasonable safeguards and shall not disclose it except to authorised representatives who need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as these terms.
Fallback wording: Fallback position requires legal approval.
Applicable documents: NDA, MSA
Escalation: Broad disclosure to affiliates or subcontractors without controls.

## RULE_004 - Termination
Policy: ABC should have a right to terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days after written notice.
Red flags:
- No termination right for material breach
- Vendor has unrestricted termination rights
- No transition or data-return obligations
Preferred wording: Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days after written notice.
Fallback wording: Fallback position requires legal approval.
Applicable documents: MSA
Escalation: Termination restrictions prevent ABC from responding to material service failure.

## RULE_005 - Indemnification
Policy: Vendor should indemnify ABC against third-party claims arising from vendor negligence, wilful misconduct, or infringement of third-party intellectual property rights.
Red flags:
- No vendor indemnification
- IP infringement excluded
- Indemnification obligations broadly excluded from liability limits without approval
Preferred wording: Vendor shall indemnify and hold harmless ABC Technologies against third-party claims arising from Vendor's negligence, wilful misconduct, or infringement of third-party intellectual property rights.
Fallback wording: Fallback position requires legal approval.
Applicable documents: MSA
Escalation: Indemnity scope or cap treatment is materially narrowed.

## RULE_006 - Governing Law
Policy: Governing law and dispute forum must be acceptable to ABC and reviewed for the transaction. The playbook does not designate a universally approved jurisdiction.
Red flags:
- Unfamiliar or commercially impractical forum
- Conflicting governing law and dispute resolution provisions
Preferred wording: Governing law and dispute resolution wording must be approved by ABC's legal team for the relevant transaction.
Fallback wording: No pre-approved fallback. Legal approval required.
Applicable documents: NDA, MSA
Escalation: Any non-standard jurisdiction or exclusive foreign forum.
